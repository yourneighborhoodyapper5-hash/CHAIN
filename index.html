IRONCLAD

a tiny combat prototype

Keyboard / Mouse

  • WASD — Move
  • Mouse — Look
  • Shift — Sprint
  • Left Click — Attack (swing right as a hit lands to parry)
  • Right Click (hold) — Block
  • Space — Dodge

Controller

  • Left Stick — Move
  • Right Stick — Camera
  • RT / R2 — Attack / parry (time it)
  • LT / L2 (hold) — Block
  • A / Cross — Sprint (hold)
  • B / Circle — Dodge

Mouse will lock to the window while playing. Press ESC to release it.
